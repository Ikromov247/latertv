1 It's live. No pausing, seeking or skipping. Tune in and you land mid-program.
2 It never stops. Channels keep airing while the app is closed.
3 Watched means gone. Once you've seen 70% of a program, it leaves the channel.
4 Commercials happen. Ads YouTube inserts may still play.
